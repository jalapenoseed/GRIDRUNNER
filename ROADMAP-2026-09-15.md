## 2. Physical power-line perch and recharge — first slice implemented in v7.20

Implemented for the existing Utility with its engineer module, after onboarding in Ghost Signal. The visual wires and flight anchors share a sampled sag model. Selection projects continuously onto each supported wire while reserving tower-end clearance, rejecting obstructed/occupied points and checking the home radio link. The drone physically approaches and aligns, displays an attachment clamp, stops motors, couples, recharges to 90% and stays parked until recalled. Pause suspends charging; save/load retains charge and circuit balances and rechecks coupling. Recall/failsafes clear the wire before return. No proximity energy or offline charge.

Two finite game circuits provide 500/1200 Wh initially; the north feed requires the existing substation relay and coupler repair. Utility's onboard capacity is 180 Wh for this mechanic, with game-time input 1.2 Wh/s. These are gameplay values. The existing receiver and trailer systems remain independent. Further slices still need wider region/circuit authoring, richer grid restoration/interference, repeated-instance occupancy and pack/trailer logistics.

The intended full system remains:

The user specifically wants physical perching on energized conductors, inspired by the supplied SDU-style references. It is **not** a generic proximity-energy aura and not limited to the existing authored receiver platform. The old receiver mechanic is still present; it is not this new system.

- Find reachable points along live transmission/distribution spans; allow perching anywhere valid on a supported span, not only a premarked station. Account for sag, clearance and occupied positions.
- State sequence: LINE DETECTED → ALIGN → PERCH → COUPLING → CHARGING → RELEASE. Drone aligns and grips the line, stops propulsion, charges while attached, then safely detaches before flight.
- Show line status, charging rate, estimated time, signal and interference. Dead lines produce no energy and encourage tracing/restoring the network. Prevent duplicate energy accounting and recheck line state after load.
- Keep electrical engineering abstract and fictionalized. This is game behavior, not real-world instructions for contacting power lines.

## 3. Harvesters, batteries and trailer logistics — first pack slice implemented in v7.21

Implemented in v7.21: up to eight uniquely owned, buildable 120 Wh / 1.2 kg removable packs; Utility/Cargo load limits; mass-sensitive campaign flight; Utility onboard recharge followed by pack charging to 90%; physical release and delivery to the current trailer position; stop-and-dwell unloading; capped reserve credit at the existing 20 Wh per reserve unit, with excess retained in the pack. Ownership survives aircraft selection, pause/cancel, safety return, save/load and Flight Yard isolation. Stored pack mass consumes trailer cargo capacity. Circuit balances never increase from unloading. Each deployed aircraft has a stable compact color beacon (Scout cyan, Cargo amber, Utility lime, Relay magenta), including thermal; thermal airframes retain a bright depth-tested heat signature.

Still planned: acquire or build multiple harvesting drones. Fly them out manually or dispatch them; leave them perched until needed. They charge their own batteries and removable carried packs, return stored energy to the trailer, unload and redeploy.

- Model charged packs, energy capacity, charge, mass and ownership explicitly. Pack jobs now visit the actual trailer for unloading, then return the empty aircraft to the bike. Other existing return commands retain bike recovery.
- Independent deployed drones persist through saving/loading. Only eligible equipment can lift a pack. Returning with heavy packs uses loaded flight and reserve estimates.
- No duplicated or disappearing packs when switching aircraft, docking, changing tasks, interrupting a return or reloading a save.

## 4. Autonomous fleet / power-management layer — first policy slice implemented in v7.22

The user wants a system-management game underneath exploration, combining scouting, relay coverage and harvesting:

- One Harvest → charge to 90% → return → unload job is implemented. v7.22 adds an explicit armed repeat/redeploy policy with 40/60/80/100% reserve targets, stored-pack availability, source depletion, weather/interference holds, damage thresholds and safe timed retries. It never silently enables itself; the player must first complete a manual delivery, then arm it.
- Scout → discover a live corridor → establish relay → harvest while idle.
- Maintain trailer reserve above a configurable threshold (example: 40%).
- Leave a charged drone/pack in place until recalled; choose between charge stockpiling and transport.
- Recall or hold safely when weather, interference, weak signal, damage or insufficient return energy threaten the task. Show why a task cannot proceed.
- Fleet view reports current jobs, reserve, generation, consumption and estimated return times. Policy thresholds are examples, not finalized balance settings.

## 6. Eclipse world state

Selectable and potentially rare event with distinctive sky/lighting, reduced solar generation, temperature/atmosphere changes, navigation visibility and authored NPC/wildlife/radio responses. Stored energy and live lines become temporarily more valuable.

- Existing night/blackout/weather modes are not an eclipse. An actual eclipse needs a time/state envelope and matching energy behavior.
- Use one shared solar-availability value for visuals and every solar generator; no sunlight-independent solar charging during totality.
- Save event phase and test entry, totality, exit, pause/resume and reload. Sensor/interference anomalies and special story clues are design possibilities, not implemented behavior.
