# v7.32 — Swarm Command

Adds **Swarm Command** as a standalone training option beside Flight Yard. It is a virtual sandbox and does not mutate expedition saves, battery, hull, discoveries or active fleet tasks.

- Program Wedge, Line, Trail, Grid, Orbit, Dual Orbit and Adaptive Scatter formations.
- Combine Hold, Orbit, Weave, Pulse and Search patterns with Survey, Escort, Relay Mesh, Perimeter, Intercept and Energy Harvest objectives.
- Choose fixed, leader, centroid or objective-relative origin logic; Scripted, Reactive, Cooperative or Distributed intelligence; and leader, pair, nearest-neighbor or mesh teammate rules.
- Place the origin and objective directly on the command field, vary all four current airframe counts, launch/redeploy the complete virtual fleet, pause it and reset it.
- Keeps the existing Scout, Cargo, Utility and Relay color identities. Program selections are device-local practice preferences.
- Adds focused deterministic coverage for fleet composition, every formation, mission-specific slots, movement, cohesion and pause authority.

The simulator models game AI only. It does not control real aircraft, train a neural policy, or claim collision-free guarantees.
